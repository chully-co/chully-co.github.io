PROJECT 01 ServiceNow · Flow Designer · Integration Hub

ServiceNow OOTB Replatform & Lifecycle Overhaul

We moved off an older, heavily customized ServiceNow instance to a clean out-of-the-box (OOTB) instance so the platform would be maintainable long-term. Working as a team of four alongside an implementation vendor over several months, we rebuilt the core service delivery layer from the ground up.

  • Workflow & Catalog Consolidation: Recreated legacy workflow engine processes in Flow Designer (100+ flows) and rebuilt the Service Catalog from scratch—consolidating 200+ legacy items down to under 100 while maintaining near feature parity.
  • Integrations & Change Management: Re-established Integration Hub spokes and redesigned the Change Management process for the new instance.
  • Employee Lifecycle Overhaul (Nearly Complete): Leading requirements scoping and coordinating a development team to migrate personnel onboarding and offboarding from a separate workflow engine into ServiceNow.

PROJECT 02 SharePoint · ShareGate · CUI Compliance

2 TB DocuShare to SharePoint Migration

I led a nine-month migration of 2 TB of company data—including Controlled Unclassified Information (CUI)—from legacy DocuShare into SharePoint Online, coordinating across 12 primary stakeholder groups and more than 20 content ownership groups.

  • Handling Tool Failures & Duplicates: DocuShare carried heavy custom metadata, and the migration tool frequently errored out and duplicated data mid-transfer. We worked through validation, deduplication, and batch retries to ensure every file and metadata record landed cleanly.
  • Stakeholder Coordination: Coordinated cutovers across 20+ ownership groups to keep operational downtime minimal while preserving strict CUI compliance boundaries.
  • Long-Term Ownership: Once the data was safely across, content owners were able to reorganize and clean up their departmental libraries in SharePoint.

PROJECT 03 SharePoint Online · Power Automate · Change Management

SharePoint Online Company Intranet & Document Automation

We needed to get internal content off an insecure public-facing website and consolidate scattered resources from WordPress, ADP, and local file shares into a single, secure SharePoint Online intranet.

  • Federated Hub Architecture: Each department maintains its own SharePoint site, with departmental news automatically rolling up to the main intranet home site alongside regular CEO Teams Talks.
  • Training & Reception: I designed and led the training program for site owners and contributors, helping departments take ownership of their pages and earning a very positive reception across the company.
  • Daily Document Management: Built a company-wide Power Automate document management workflow that remains in active daily use with minimal bug fixes required since launch.
